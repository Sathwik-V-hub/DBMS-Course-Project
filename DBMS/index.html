SQL Queries & Analytical Reports

1. Daily Attendance Summary Report

SELECT e.name, a.att_date, a.check_in, a.check_out, a.status FROM attendance a JOIN employee e ON a.emp_id = e.emp_id;

2. Approved Leave Days Per Employee

SELECT e.name, SUM(DATEDIFF(end_date, start_date)+1) FROM leave_request WHERE status='Approved' GROUP BY e.emp_id;
